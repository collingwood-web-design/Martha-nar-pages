Frequently Asked Questions

Who are these recovery tools for?

These tools are designed for adults recovering from narcissistic abuse, emotional manipulation, gaslighting, trauma bonding, and complex relational trauma who are ready for structured, self-paced support.

What format are the tools in?

Each recovery tool is a guided audio recording you can listen to on your phone, tablet, or computer — privately, at your own pace, as often as you need.

How is this different from books or free videos?

Books and videos help you understand what happened. These tools are designed to help your nervous system do the work — regulation, stabilization, and integration — in a structured recovery sequence.

Do I need to be in therapy to use these?

No. These tools can be used on their own or alongside therapy. They are educational self-help resources and are not a substitute for psychotherapy when clinical care is needed.
